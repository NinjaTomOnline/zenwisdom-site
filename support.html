Contact

Use email for account-free support, bug reports, or launch questions. If you are reporting an issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version, and the screen you were using when the problem happened.

Response time

Usually within 2 to 3 business days

If your note is about a blocking issue, mention that in the subject line so it is easier to triage quickly.

Best details to include

  • What you expected to happen
  • What happened instead
  • Whether the issue affects quotes, meditation, journal, favorites, or profile settings
  • Any screenshots or screen recordings that make the problem easier to reproduce

Troubleshooting

Common things to check first

No meditation audio

Confirm your iPhone is not muted, media volume is up, and the track is not paused. Zen Wisdom uses bundled audio, so playback issues are usually local device-state problems rather than streaming errors.

Favorites look empty

Favorites are stored locally on the device in the current build. If you reinstall the app or move to a different device, that local state does not automatically follow you.

Journal changes did not appear

Make sure the reflection body is not empty before saving. Journal history is written locally, and each saved note should appear in the day-by-day history section right away.

Premium access questions

Premium access uses Apple purchase and restore flows for Weekly, Annual, and Lifetime access. If premium access does not update after purchase or restore, include the plan you selected and whether the issue happened in TestFlight, sandbox, or the live App Store.
